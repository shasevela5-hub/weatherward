Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI analysis work?

Our advanced AI uses computer vision to detect clothing items, analyze colors, patterns, and styles. It then provides personalized recommendations based on your preferences and current weather conditions.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes, we take privacy seriously. All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest. You have full control over your data and can delete your account anytime with complete data removal.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes! The app includes offline mode so you can access your recent scans and recommendations without an internet connection. Data syncs automatically when you're back online.

What Android versions are supported?

The app is optimized for Android 8.0 and above. We continuously test and optimize for the latest Android versions to ensure the best performance.

Is there a premium subscription?

Yes, we offer a premium subscription with advanced AI analysis, unlimited scans, priority support, and exclusive features. A free version is also available with basic functionality.

Can I share outfits with friends?

Absolutely! You can share outfits directly to social media, or invite friends to view your profile and get feedback on your styling choices.
